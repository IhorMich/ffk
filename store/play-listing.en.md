## Full description

TEMPO is a match diary for one young footballer, for a parent on the sideline. Coach mode is separate.

Tap Start and the big pitch buttons open. Goals, passes, tackles and mistakes move the rating live. It starts at 6.0 and finishes between 0 and 10. After the game, share a card in the family chat. Match types: league, friendly, cup and tournament.

Free keeps one player, and that season stays on this phone. Before you change phones, open Settings and tap “On this phone”. The file goes to Downloads / TEMPO.

Email or Google sign-in is for a coach and for a parent invited to a child’s card. Without Pro, the personal season is not uploaded.

TEMPO Pro is described in the app: up to 32 players, charts and sync between phones. It is not for sale in Google Play yet.

## What’s new
